# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our commitment / 我们的承诺

We want participation in X Clipboard to be respectful, useful, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

我们希望 X Clipboard 社区尊重差异、交流有益，并让不同背景和经验水平的人都能安心参与。

## Expected behavior / 预期行为

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback about ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Welcome corrections and make room for different perspectives.
- Protect privacy: do not publish another person's clipboard contents, credentials, or identifying information.

- 尊重他人；讨论分歧时先假设对方善意。
- 针对想法和代码提供可执行反馈，不攻击个人。
- 接受纠正，也给不同观点留出空间。
- 保护隐私：不要公开他人的剪贴板内容、凭据或身份信息。

## Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受的行为

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

骚扰、歧视、威胁、人肉搜索、未经同意的性化关注，以及持续扰乱交流的行为均不可接受。

## Reporting / 报告问题

Contact the project maintainers through the repository's private contact channel if one is configured. If no private channel is available, open an issue without including sensitive personal details and ask for a private follow-up. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible.

如仓库配置了维护者私密联系渠道，请通过该渠道报告。若没有私密渠道，可提交不含敏感个人信息的 Issue，并请求私下跟进。维护者会尽可能及时、公正地处理。
